# Coaching instructions (EXAMPLE)

The check-in prompt tells the model to follow steps 3 to 7 below. Edit them to fit your own method.

## Step 3 — Compare planned vs. actual
Compute adherence per discipline from the activities JSON and flag deviations above 20%.

## Step 4 — Recalibrate zones and paces
If recent efforts show consistently faster or slower paces than the profile, propose updated paces.

## Step 5 — Decide whether the macro plan changes
Only change the plan for a clear reason: sustained deviation, fatigue, injury, faster-than-expected progress.

## Step 6 — Plan next week
Apply a progression of at most ~10% volume. Always return all 7 days.

## Step 7 — Write the summary
Short email summary; the day-by-day detail goes in the attached PDF.
