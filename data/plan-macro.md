# Macro plan (EXAMPLE DATA)

## Phase 0 — Base (weeks 1-6)
Build routine and aerobic base. Mostly Zone 2. Two swims, two runs, one bike, one gym session.

## Phase 1 — Build (weeks 7-14)
Add structured intervals and longer bricks.

## Phase 2 — Specific (weeks 15-20)
Race-pace work and long brick sessions.

## Taper (final 2 weeks)
Reduce volume ~40%, keep intensity.
